# CSUI Course Visualizer

[course.csui.dev](https://course.csui.dev/) is an interactive web-based curriculum map designed for students and academic advisors at Fasilkom Universitas Indonesia. It provides an intuitive, zoomable, and searchable visual graph of study programs, making academic planning clear and straightforward.

The platform visualizes courses as interactive cards and prerequisite rules as directed paths. By selecting a course, users can instantly see both its upstream dependencies—what courses it is a prerequisite of—and its downstream pathway—what courses it is a prerequisite for. 

With built-in support for different study programs, credit tracking, and detailed course descriptions, the visualizer simplifies degree planning and helps students understand the structural relationships of their academic journey.
